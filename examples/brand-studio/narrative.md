# brand-studio

brand-studio is a Claude Code plugin that gives your app a brand, for developers who know how to build software but not how to design an identity. Branding an app is hard, and asking a model for "an SVG logo" gives you circles, gradients and broken letters, with nobody mentioning copyright or font licences.

brand-studio works like a careful designer at your side: it turns your app's story into one idea, draws three concepts in black, proves them with checks and renders before you see them, lets you pick once, and builds a full kit (mark, wordmark, palette, type, icons) by editing your pick. Every rule has a number and a source.

You get a strong starting identity and get back to building your app. It is a first draft, not a replacement for a designer.
