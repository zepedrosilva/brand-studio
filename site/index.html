A brand identity for your app, checked before you see it.

You can build an app, but you have never designed an identity for one. Ask a model for a logo and you get circles, gradients and broken letters, with no word about who owns them.

brand-studio is a Claude Code plugin that works the way a careful designer does. It turns a few paragraphs about your project into a mark, wordmark, lockups, palette, exports and a brand sheet, all as SVG, each checked against numbered, sourced rules.

Install

/plugin marketplace add zepedrosilva/plugins
/plugin install brand-studio@zepedrosilva
/brand-studio:brand-create <your project's story>

At the start of a run, the plugin checks for its optional tools and offers to install what is missing. Say no and it still runs, judging the rules by reading the files.

How it works

  1. Your story becomes a brief, with a positioning line and a word to own.
  2. A shortlist of metaphors, with the clichés of your category named.
  3. Three greyscale concepts, drawn in parallel and checked.
  4. An independent review, then you pick one.
  5. The kit is built by editing your pick: palette, wordmark, lockups and icons.
  6. Exports, a page that shows the kit in use, and a brand sheet with legal warnings.
