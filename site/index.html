A brand identity for your app, checked before you see it. A Claude Code plugin for developers without a design background: mark, wordmark, palette, exports and a brand sheet, all as SVG.

Install 
/plugin marketplace add zepedrosilva/plugins
/plugin install brand-studio@zepedrosilva

At the start of a run it checks for its optional tools and offers to install what's missing. Say no and it still runs, judging the rules by reading the files.

Examples
Make an identity from your project's story
/brand-studio:brand-create Tidepool is a small app for wild swimmers.
It shows tide times, water temperature and who else is swimming nearby.
It should feel calm and outdoorsy, not sporty.
Or point at a file
/brand-studio:brand-create README.md
Ask in plain words
Make a logo for this project.
I need a favicon and an app icon for my side project.
Review a logo you already have
/brand-studio:brand-review assets/logo.svg
/brand-studio:brand-review brand/
How it works
  1. Brief. Your story becomes a brief with a positioning line and a word to own. It asks once for anything missing, with a suggested answer.
  2. Shortlist. Metaphor lines, with the clichés of your category named.
  3. Three concepts, drawn in greyscale in parallel and checked with scripts and renders.
  4. One checkpoint. A reviewer on a different model checks all three; you pick one.
  5. The kit is built by editing your pick: palette, wordmark, lockups and icons.
  6. Exports, a page that shows the kit in use, and a brand sheet with legal warnings.
